Studio Form - Custom Enterprise AI & Voice Agent Company in Indore

Headquartered in Indore, India, Studio Form is a leading global developer of custom agentic artificial intelligence solutions. We specialize in building and deploying production-ready autonomous systems, multilingual voice agents, private domain-specific Large Language Models (LLMs), and layout-aware document intelligence architectures for enterprises across India and international markets. Our engineered platforms automate repetitive workflows, reduce overhead, and deliver measurable return on investment for operations in healthcare, logistics, e-commerce, banking, hospitality, real estate, and education.

Autonomous AI Voice Agents & Contact Center Automation

Our flagship AI Voice Agents represent the state of the art in conversational telephony. Built with advanced Natural Language Understanding (NLU), they understand and communicate in major world languages as well as local Indic dialects like Hindi, Marathi, Bengali, and Tamil. We deploy inbound reception agents that handle appointment bookings and route complex customer inquiries, outbound qualifier systems that nurture leads directly from CRM platforms, and e-commerce transaction bots that verify orders to reduce Return-to-Origin (RTO) rates. Every deployment is customized with secure database integrations, live status logging, and automatic email or SMS event notifications.

Custom LLM Development & Fine-Tuning

In the field of model training and fine-tuning, Studio Form helps organizations bypass public API restrictions. Whether you require a specialized model for medical diagnostics, legal contract analysis, or financial risk assessment, we optimize models like Claude, GPT-4, Llama 3, and Gemini to align with your proprietary data. Enterprise AI Automation & Agent Workflows

For document-heavy workflows, we build custom Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems. Unlike simple PDF search boxes, our layout-aware parsers extract complex tables, headers, footers, and charts from structured and unstructured documents. This is critical for high-stakes fields like healthcare billing, corporate tax audits, and compliance reviews. Our RAG platforms connect to vector databases like Pinecone, Qdrant, or MongoDB Atlas, employing hybrid search and reranking models to deliver factual, zero-hallucination answers.
